a rubber duck derby — eighty ducks, one river, no engines

HOW TO RACE

THE RIVER

You can't outswim the current — only read it. The middle runs fastest. Dark glassy tongues with chevrons are fast chutes. Pale sandbars and brown mud kill your speed. Reeds are slack water.

MOVES

WASD/←↑↓→ or stick: rhythmic paddle strokes. SHIFT/A: splash shove — slam nearby ducks away (4s cooldown). Weight class changes your size, power, and how hard you hit.

HAZARDS

Rocks stop you. Logs and booms block lanes — aim for the gap. Eddies and the whirlpool trap drifters. Watch for the lunging pike, the snapping turtle, and each course's machinery. Islands split the river: one lane is fast and mean, one is safe and slow.

RACES

Every course is a closed circuit — a lap is crossing the start/finish line. The glowing rune-water is a magic surge channel: it runs uphill at full race speed. Lap 2 runs high water; lap 3 opens a flood-gate shortcut. Once a lap the dam releases a surge wave — ride it. Rivals remember: beat your NEMESIS.
