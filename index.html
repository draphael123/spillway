HOW TO RACE

THE RIVER

You can't outswim the current — only read it. The middle runs fastest. Dark glassy tongues with chevrons are fast chutes. Pale sandbars and brown mud kill your speed. Reeds are slack water.

MOVES

WASD/←↑↓→ or stick: rhythmic paddle strokes. SHIFT/A: splash shove — slam nearby ducks away. Hold clean fast-water lines to charge Flow, then press E/CTRL or controller B for a tactical burst. C changes camera. ESC/START pauses the race.

HAZARDS

Rocks stop you. Logs and booms block lanes — aim for the gap. Eddies and the whirlpool trap drifters. Watch for the lunging pike, the snapping turtle, and each course's machinery. Islands split the river: one lane is fast and mean, one is safe and slow.

RACES

Every course is a closed circuit. Each has its own powered climb — Rune Slalom, Pressure Pump, Glowroot Hunt, Sap-Rise Launch, Faucet Jet, or Lock Elevator — with a different pulse and steering rhythm. Lap 2 runs high water; lap 3 opens a flood-gate shortcut. Once a lap the dam releases a surge wave. Rivals remember: beat your NEMESIS.
